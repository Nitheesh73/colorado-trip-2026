Indian food along the route

Picked for Guntur-style tastes — places that can go properly spicy on request. Small mountain towns (Alamosa, Leadville) have no Indian kitchens, so plan a home-style meal or snacks those nights.

Colorado Springs

Day 1–2
  • Turmeric Indian Cuisine★ 4.9 (818)
    5535 Powers Center Point
    Ask for South Indian spice level — they'll turn it up
  • Mausam Indian Flavors★ 4.8 (1,826)
    3167 W Colorado Ave
    Good dosa and idli selection

Alamosa

Night 1
No Indian kitchens here — it's a small town. Best bet: pack a tiffin box from Colorado Springs, or try Sushi Thai Alamosa (4.7) if the group wants something with real heat.
